NABD · 07 · The engine

Three signals go in.

Five steps. One loop.

  1. 1
    Body: how did she sleep and recover?Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin and Samsung through Apple Health and Health Connect, plus WHOOP.
  2. 2
    Plate: what did she eat?One photo. The food model knows kabsa, mandi, dates and local chains.
  3. 3
    Gym: what did she do?Check-ins, classes and PT sessions from the gym's own app.
  4. 4
    Nudge: what should she do next?NABD scores readiness and drop-off risk. The coach says one clear thing, in Arabic or English.
  5. 5
    Reward: make it stickStreaks for sleeping and eating well, not just showing up. Free classes and partner perks.
